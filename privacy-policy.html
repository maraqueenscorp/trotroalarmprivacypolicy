Privacy Policy

What the app does

Trotro Alarm helps passengers on trotros, buses, trains and taxis wake up before their stop. You choose a destination, and the app uses your phone's GPS on the device to measure the distance to that destination and sound an alarm nearby.

Data stored on your phone

Your chosen destinations, trip history, language and alarm-sound preferences are stored locally on your phone only using on-device local storage. Maraqueens Corporation does not operate a server for this app, does not use Firebase, requires no account, no login and no sign-up, and does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There is no advertising tracking or analytics tracking in the app.

GPS / location

GPS is used on-device to calculate the distance between you and your destination (Haversine formula). Your live GPS track used for the alarm is not uploaded to Maraqueens Corporation — we have no server to receive it. You can turn GPS or location permission off at any time in Android Settings; the alarm then cannot measure distance.

Important disclosure: third-party map & search servers

Place search uses the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) and maps use OpenStreetMap map tiles (and the Leaflet library). When you search or view a map, those requests go directly from your phone to those third-party servers and may transmit your search terms, IP address and the map coordinates you view. Those services have their own privacy policies (OpenStreetMap Foundation). For this reason we do not claim that literally all data stays on your device: search and uncached map-tile requests contact third-party servers. Once a destination is selected, its coordinates are kept on your phone and offline GPS distance monitoring does not need those servers.

Permissions

Location (for GPS distance), Vibration (for the wake-up alarm), Wake Lock / keep-screen-on (while the Sleeping screen is shown), Internet (only for search and map tiles) and Notifications / foreground-service location where applicable for reliable background operation.

Children, changes & contact

The app is a general travel utility and does not knowingly collect data from children. If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Questions: maraqueenscorp@gmail.com.
